Show Off Your AcreetionOS Setup

Download the official FastFetch config to display your system info with the AcreetionOS logo and green accent theme. Take a screenshot and share it on r/unixporn or r/linux!

# One command to download, install, and run:
curl -sL https://acreetionos.org/acreetionos-fastfetch.jsonc -o ~/.config/fastfetch/config.jsonc &&
sudo pacman -S --noconfirm fastfetch &&
fastfetch
