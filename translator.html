Document Translator

Paste a link to any public webpage or text document and turn it into a readable translation while keeping its headings, lists, links, and code.

Only public links are supported. Password-protected pages, PDFs, images, and pages that require JavaScript cannot be read. Document text is sent to the project's free AI provider chain for translation.
