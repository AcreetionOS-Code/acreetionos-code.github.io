Run a mirror

We publish three x86_64 ISO images (about 7.2 GB total) plus SHA256SUMS.txt, rebuilt weekly on Sundays under the same filenames. The rsync source is public and read-only: rsync://us.iso.acreetionos.org/iso_images/ -- the same files are also served over HTTPS at iso.acreetionos.org/acreetion/ if you prefer to sync over HTTP. A daily sync is more than enough. If you set one up, tell us at natalie@acreetionos.org and we will register you on this page and in the download steering. AcreetionOS is free, takes no money, and collects no data from anyone -- mirrors are how a small project reaches people far from our one US server.

Manual fallbacks (MEGA and Google Drive) are listed on the download page.
