Run a mirror

We publish three x86_64 ISO images (about 7.2 GB total) plus SHA256SUMS.txt, rebuilt weekly on Sundays under the same filenames. The rsync source is public and read-only: rsync://us.iso.acreetionos.org/iso_images/ -- the same files are also served over HTTPS at iso.acreetionos.org/acreetion/ if you prefer to sync over HTTP. A daily sync is more than enough. If you set one up, register it with the form below -- it goes on this page as soon as our checker confirms it is live and in sync. Questions go to registermirror@acreetionos.org. AcreetionOS is free, takes no money, and collects no data from anyone -- mirrors are how a small project reaches people far from our one US server.

Register your mirror

Once your mirror is synced, fill this in. Our worker fetches your SHA256SUMS.txt, checks it against the current release, and confirms the ISOs are really there at the right size. If the check passes, your mirror goes on this page immediately. We re-check twice a week, and a mirror that fails two checks in a row comes off the page. Questions: registermirror@acreetionos.org.

Manual fallbacks (MEGA and Google Drive) are listed on the download page.
