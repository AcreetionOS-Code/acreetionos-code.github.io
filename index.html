Welcome to AcreetionOS

AcreetionOS is an Arch-based Linux distribution designed for stability, ease of use, and a commitment to building software that makes the world a better place. We believe in transparency, technical integrity, and the power of open-source community.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between the power and flexibility of Arch and the simplicity that everyday computer users expect. We aim to make the Arch ecosystem accessible to a wider audience—from students and developers to casual desktop users—by providing a stable, pre-configured, and beautiful desktop experience right out of the box.

Under the Hood

AcreetionOS is more than just a themed version of Arch Linux. We've made several key decisions and customizations to improve the user experience, stability, and performance.

Boot Process: Simple & Reliable

We use a combination of bootloaders to ensure a smooth experience. The live environment is booted using systemd-boot and syslinux for maximum hardware compatibility. For the final installation, we use grub, a powerful and widely-supported bootloader. This hybrid approach ensures that AcreetionOS boots reliably on a wide range of hardware, including both modern UEFI systems and older BIOS-based machines. Don't worry about the technical details - it just works.

Filesystem: EXT4 for Stability

We use the EXT4 filesystem by default. While newer filesystems exist, EXT4 is the most mature, stable, and well-tested filesystem in the Linux world. It offers excellent performance for desktop use cases and is incredibly reliable, ensuring your data is safe. This choice reflects our commitment to providing a stable and worry-free experience.

Audio: The Power of Pipewire

AcreetionOS uses Pipewire as its audio server, a modern replacement for PulseAudio. Pipewire is superior in every way: it offers lower latency, better CPU utilization, and seamless support for both consumer and professional audio applications. It also unifies video and audio streams, providing a more robust multimedia experience.

Using Your Own Flashing Tool

After downloading an ISO, you will need to write it to a USB drive (8GB or larger recommended). This will erase all data on the USB drive.

Balena Etcher (Recommended for most users)

Balena Etcher is a simple graphical tool for Windows, macOS, and Linux. 1. Select the downloaded AcreetionOS ISO file. 2. Select your USB drive. 3. Click "Flash!".

Ventoy (for multi-boot USBs)

Ventoy is a tool that lets you create a bootable USB drive where you can simply copy multiple ISO files. 1. Install Ventoy to your USB drive once. 2. Drag and drop the AcreetionOS ISO file onto the USB. 3. Boot from the USB to see the Ventoy menu.

Using `dd` (Advanced Linux Users)

The `dd` command is a powerful but dangerous tool. Incorrect usage can lead to data loss. Be certain of your USB drive's device name (e.g., `/dev/sdb`, not `/dev/sdb1`).

sudo dd if=/path/to/AcreetionOS.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress oflag=sync

Replace `/path/to/AcreetionOS.iso` with the actual path to your downloaded file and `/dev/sdX` with your USB device identifier.

Why We Don't Take Your Money

We've had people ask, but we haven't taken any donations. Why? To put it simply, we are developers, not accountants. Accepting donations would require us to register as a non-profit organization, which involves significant legal and administrative overhead. This includes incorporation fees, yearly compliance paperwork, and complex tax filings. These tasks would divert our limited time and resources away from what we do best: building a great Linux distribution.

More importantly, it would change the nature of our project. We want to remain a community-driven project, where decisions are made based on what's best for the users, not on financial incentives. Becoming a corporate entity, even a non-profit one, would introduce a level of bureaucracy that could stifle innovation and distract us from our core mission.

So, How Can You Help?

Instead of donating money, you can help us by reaching out to potential sponsors for open-source projects. We need resources like mirrors, hardware for testing, and other infrastructure to grow. By helping us build relationships with these sponsors, you can make a much bigger impact than a monetary donation.

If you have connections to companies that sponsor open-source projects, please get in touch with us. Your help in this area will allow us to grow sustainably and stay true to our community-focused mission.

Where did the GNOME Edition go?

We have retired the GNOME edition of AcreetionOS.

This decision was made because the future of the GNOME desktop is centered around Wayland, which is not yet complete and has some unresolved issues that impact the user experience we want to provide.

Furthermore, the GNOME Foundation is retiring the X11 session, which has been the fallback for many distributions. Continuing to support a desktop environment that is in such a state of transition is not feasible for our team at this time.

We may reconsider a GNOME edition in the future when Wayland is more mature and stable.
