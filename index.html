Welcome to AcreetionOS

AcreetionOS is a user-friendly, privacy-focused Linux distribution based on Arch Linux. It features the Cinnamon desktop environment, XLibre stability, and a curated selection of tools designed for performance and repository sovereignty. It is the ideal choice for users seeking Arch Linux power without the configuration complexity.

Under the Hood

AcreetionOS is more than just a themed version of Arch Linux. We've made several key decisions and customizations to improve the user experience, stability, and performance.

Why XLibre over Wayland?

Notice: We are migrating to XLibre in calendar year 2026. We are in the middle of this migration, and you can find the latest editions in our downloads section.

AcreetionOS prioritizes the XLibre foundation (based on X11) over the current industry push for Wayland. We believe that while Wayland is promising, it still lacks the absolute reliability required for professional and production workflows. Many users still face issues with screen sharing, global keyboard shortcuts, and compatibility with specialized hardware or NVIDIA legacy drivers. By sticking with XLibre, we ensure a stable, mature environment where your software "just works" without the configuration headaches currently common in Wayland-based compositors.

Boot Process: Simple & Reliable

We use a combination of bootloaders to ensure a smooth experience. The live environment is booted using systemd-boot and syslinux for maximum hardware compatibility. For the final installation, we use grub, a powerful and widely-supported bootloader. This hybrid approach ensures that AcreetionOS boots reliably on a wide range of hardware, including both modern UEFI systems and older BIOS-based machines.

Filesystem: EXT4 for Stability

We use the EXT4 filesystem by default. While newer filesystems exist, EXT4 is the most mature, stable, and well-tested filesystem in the Linux world. It offers excellent performance for desktop use cases and is incredibly reliable, ensuring your data is safe.

Audio: The Power of Pipewire

AcreetionOS uses Pipewire as its audio server, a modern replacement for PulseAudio. Pipewire is superior in every way: it offers lower latency, better CPU utilization, and seamless support for both consumer and professional audio applications.

Why We Don't Take Your Money

We are developers, not accountants. Accepting donations would require legal overhead that diverts time from building the OS. Instead, help us by finding sponsors for mirrors and hardware.

Retired GNOME Edition

The GNOME desktop project has shifted its focus heavily toward Wayland. Because AcreetionOS currently prioritizes the stability and broad compatibility of X11 and XLibre environments, we have made the decision to retire the GNOME edition for the time being.

This is not necessarily a permanent change. GNOME may return to AcreetionOS one day, but not until official Wayland-based variants of our OS are released. We believe in providing an experience that "just works," and we won't bring GNOME back until we can guarantee it meets our standards for performance and reliability on modern display protocols.
