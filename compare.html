* While Arch Linux has a vast knowledge base (the Arch Wiki is legendary), its community is known for a "RTFM" (Read The Fine Manual) culture that can be intimidating to newcomers. AcreetionOS was built to bridge that gap — we provide the same powerful Arch foundation with a welcoming, beginner-friendly community that helps rather than gate-keeps. Our mission: keep the power, lose the attitude.
