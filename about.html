About Natalie

Natalie is a systems administrator and the lead developer of AcreetionOS. With a focus on stability, privacy, and user sovereignty, Natalie has spent years refining the Arch Linux experience to be accessible without compromising on technical depth.

The Vision for AcreetionOS

AcreetionOS was born from a need for a Linux distribution that respects the user as much as it respects the machine. It isn't just about a pretty desktop; it's about repository sovereignty, predictable stability (XLibre), and a system that works for the user, not the other way around.

Expertise & Experience

As a seasoned systems administrator, Natalie specializes in high-performance computing, network security, and open-source infrastructure. This expertise is baked into every aspect of AcreetionOS—from the custom Wayland compositor to the optimized kernel configurations.

Location: Spokane, WA

Operating out of Spokane, Washington, Natalie is dedicated to building a local and global community of developers who value privacy and open-source integrity.
