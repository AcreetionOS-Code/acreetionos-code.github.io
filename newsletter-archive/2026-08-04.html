August 04, 2026

Daily AcreetionOS Update - August 04, 2026

Our development team has been busy refining our automated deployment pipelines. Darren recently implemented a new process to publish ISOs directly to the public server via SSH. This improvement ensures that our stable builds reach users much faster.

We are also expanding our infrastructure capabilities with the initial commit of the Proxmox image. This addition makes it easier for users to deploy our ecosystem within virtualized environments. We are building a foundation that is truly built to last.

Natalie has been working on the website workers to ensure smoother performance. Recent updates have optimized our routing for free models on the main site. These small tweaks make the digital experience much more responsive for everyone.

The community has been buzzing with significant news from the open source world. We are celebrating twenty years of Pandoc and the amazing work of its creator. It is inspiring to see such longevity in the tools we use every day.

We are also keeping a close watch on security developments in the industry. A recent JFrog report discusses whether recent SQLite vulnerability reports are genuine or just noise. Staying informed helps us keep AcreetionOS secure and reliable.

Another notable release is the new version of C-Kermit. This marks a major milestone for the project after many years of stability. We love seeing these essential pieces of history continue to evolve.

For those looking to get involved, our community Discord is the best place to start. You can find support and chat with the core developers there. Join us as we continue to build something meaningful together.

A quick tip for our builders is to explore the new ISO output settings. You can now direct your builds straight to your host documents folder. This makes managing your local testing environment much simpler.

Thank you for being part of the AcreetionOS journey. We are proud to be building freedom into Linux one commit at a time. Have a wonderful day and happy computing.
