AcreetionOS Update — July 02, 2026

July 02, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has just seen another round of refinements, building on the recent introduction of per-pipeline staging directories and relative symlinks for the latest.iso file, which have significantly improved the reliability of our ISO build process. These changes, detailed on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html, demonstrate the community's ongoing commitment to streamlining our release process.

Development The focus on enhancing our CI pipeline is part of a broader effort to ensure that AcreetionOS remains a robust and efficient Arch-based distribution, leveraging the strengths of the Arch repos and tooling like pacman. By addressing potential bottlenecks and inconsistencies in our build process, we're laying the groundwork for more consistent and reliable releases. For those interested in the technical details, our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html provides a comprehensive overview of our development process.

Community The community has been active in discussing recent updates, including the release of Emacs 31.1 and the latest stable kernel set, which includes versions like 7.1.10 and 6.18.46. These discussions, often hosted on our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html, showcase the diverse interests and expertise within our community. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting to explore Linux, there's always something new to learn and contribute. For the latest news and updates, you can also check out our flash page at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html.

Tip of the day For users looking to get the most out of AcreetionOS, exploring the customization options available through our documentation and wiki guides can be incredibly rewarding. From tweaking desktop environments to optimizing system performance, there are numerous ways to tailor AcreetionOS to your specific needs. By visiting https://acreetionos.org/, you can find more information on getting started and contributing to the project.
