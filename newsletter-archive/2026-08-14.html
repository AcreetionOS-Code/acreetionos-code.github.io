Daily AcreetionOS Update - August 14, 2026

Dear AcreetionOS community,

We're excited to share the latest developments from the AcreetionOS project. Our community has been actively contributing to the growth and improvement of our sovereign Linux distribution. We'd like to highlight a few recent commits and news articles that showcase the progress we've made.

In the past week, we've seen a surge in community engagement, with several new commits and contributions to our codebase. Natalie has made significant progress on the hyperpick project, which aims to help users pick the right hypervisor for testing OS ISOs. This feature is a crucial step in ensuring our distribution is thoroughly tested and reliable.

Meanwhile, cobra3282000 has been working on resolving conflicts in our geocode-glib update, ensuring that our systems are up-to-date and secure. Their dedication to maintaining the stability of our distribution is invaluable to our community.

We're also pleased to see our project featured in several news articles, highlighting the ease of use and flexibility of AcreetionOS. BetaNews has published a piece on our distribution, praising its user-friendly interface and seamless experience. We're grateful for the recognition and look forward to continuing to provide a top-notch experience for our users.

In other news, Shelly 3.0.4, a GUI package manager for Arch Linux, has been updated with a new search experience. This development is a testament to the collaborative spirit of our community and the Arch ecosystem.

We'd like to extend our gratitude to all our contributors, including Domas, who has been working on bypassing memory protection with AMD's memory controllers. Their dedication to advancing the state of Linux is truly commendable.

As we continue to grow and improve, we're committed to maintaining our core values of sovereignty, transparency, and community-driven development. We're proud to be a part of the Linux community and look forward to continuing to work together to create a better, more secure, and more user-friendly distribution.

If you're interested in getting involved or learning more about AcreetionOS, we invite you to join our Discord server or explore our GitHub repository. Your contributions and feedback are invaluable to our project's success.

Thank you for being part of the AcreetionOS community. We're excited to see what the future holds for our distribution and look forward to continuing to work together to make it the best it can be.

Best regards, Natalie Cole-Clift Spiva and Darren Clift
