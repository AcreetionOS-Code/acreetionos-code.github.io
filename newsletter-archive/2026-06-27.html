Your Daily Dose of AcreetionOS: Privacy, Power, and Pipewire!

June 27, 2026

Hello AcreetionOS Community,

Welcome to another day of rolling release goodness! We hope your systems are running smoothly and your audio is crystal clear. Today, we want to shine a spotlight on one of the core pillars of AcreetionOS: your absolute system sovereignty.

In a world where data is constantly being harvested, we believe your computer should work for you, not against you. That is why AcreetionOS comes pre-configured with privacy in mind. But did you know you can take it a step further right now? By default, we use the EXT4 filesystem for rock-solid reliability and XLibre for a highly stable graphical experience, meaning fewer background processes phoning home. To boost your privacy today, open your terminal and check your active network connections using the ss command. It is a quick, easy way to see exactly what is talking to the outside world, ensuring you remain the sole captain of your digital ship.

On the development front, our team has been hard at work refining the Cinnamon desktop experience. We are currently tweaking the default panel applets to make your daily workflow even smoother in the upcoming update. We love the Cinnamon desktop because it provides a traditional, intuitive layout without sacrificing the cutting-edge packages that Arch Linux is famous for.

If you are currently wrestling with a more complex distribution and just want things to work beautifully out of the box, now is a great time to switch. AcreetionOS gives you the bleeding-edge software of Arch, the rock-solid stability of XLibre and EXT4, and the superior audio handling of Pipewire, all wrapped in a genuinely beginner-friendly package.

Thank you for being part of our growing community. Keep rolling, keep exploring, and as always, keep your systems sovereign.

Happy computing,

The AcreetionOS Team
