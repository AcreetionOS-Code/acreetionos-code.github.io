AcreetionOS Update — June 11, 2026

June 11, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has just been fine-tuned to use relative symlinks for the latest ISO images, ensuring that they resolve correctly on the host web root, and this change is now live and documented on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html. This improvement, combined with the recent introduction of per-pipeline staging directories, significantly enhances the reliability and timeliness of our release process.

Development The development team continues to review and incorporate upstream changes from Arch Linux, ensuring that AcreetionOS remains current and secure. Recent security updates, including those for ansible-core, cups-filters, and curl, have been applied, and the latest stable kernel set, including versions 7.1.10 and 6.1.18, is now available. For more information on our development process and the latest changes, visit our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html.

Community The community has been active, with discussions around the recent release of Emacs 31.1 and its implications for AcreetionOS users. Additionally, our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html has been updated to reflect the latest changes and best practices for using AcreetionOS. As always, we invite users to explore our flash page at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html for the latest news and updates on the project.
