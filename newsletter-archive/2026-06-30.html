AcreetionOS Update — June 30, 2026

June 30, 2026

Our continuous integration pipeline just got a significant boost with the introduction of per-pipeline staging directories, which will help avoid publish races between concurrent builds, and the use of relative symlinks for the latest.iso file, ensuring it resolves correctly on the host web root. This improvement is a direct result of our community's efforts to refine the ISO build process, and you can track the latest changes on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html.

Development The recent updates to our CI pipeline have been instrumental in improving the reliability of our ISO build process, and we're excited to see the impact it will have on our release process. As an Arch-based distribution, we're committed to providing the latest and greatest software packages, and our pipeline tweaks will help us achieve that goal. For more information on our development process, you can visit our wiki page at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html.

Community We're grateful for the contributions from our community members, who have been actively working on refining our pipeline and improving our overall workflow. The release of Emacs 31.1 is also a significant milestone, and we're excited to see the new features and improvements it brings. If you're interested in learning more about Emacs or other software packages available in AcreetionOS, you can check out our documentation page at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html. Additionally, you can find the latest security updates and kernel sets on our website, including the recent 7.1.10 and 6.18.46 kernel releases, which can be accessed through https://acreetionos.org/flash.html.
