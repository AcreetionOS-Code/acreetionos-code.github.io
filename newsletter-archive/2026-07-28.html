Daily AcreetionOS Update -- July 28, 2026

July 28, 2026

Daily AcreetionOS Update - July 28, 2026. Hello AcreetionOS community, here's what's new today.

This week we added secret detection to the .gitlab-ci.yml files across all repositories, ensuring that any accidental credentials are caught early. We also introduced SAST scanning to the CI pipeline, strengthening our security posture before builds. Additionally, the initial commit for the cinnamon-settings-configs repository was made, paving the way for further desktop customizations.

The kernel prepatch 7.2-rc5 is now available for testing, and we restored worker routes in wrangler.toml to improve Cloudflare integration. Our CI pipelines have been streamlined with background steps and OIDC custom properties, reducing build times. These changes reflect our ongoing effort to keep AcreetionOS stable and secure.

Community buzz is high as BetaNews highlighted Microsoft’s Windows 11 start menu overhaul and a major update to ReactOS, both showcasing the dynamic Linux and alternative OS landscape. Meanwhile, the announcement of AnduinOS 1.3 beta invites users to explore a new, user‑friendly distribution. These stories underscore the vibrant ecosystem surrounding AcreetionOS.

Nobara 42’s switch from Firefox to Brave and its claim as the friendliest Fedora‑based distro have sparked lively discussion among users. The recent release of GNU Binutils 2.47 and Monday’s security updates also remind us of the continuous improvements happening across the open‑source world. Staying informed helps us all contribute to a healthier, more secure Linux community.

A handy tip for contributors is to enable secret detection in your local .gitlab-ci.yml to catch credential leaks before they reach the main branch. This practice not only protects our codebase but also reduces the workload for the maintainers during security reviews.

Darren continues to drive the heavy lifting with Calamares customizations, ISO builds, and CI/CD pipeline maintenance, while Natalie focuses on the polished Cinnamon desktop experience. Their complementary strengths make the two‑person team a true miracle in the open‑source world. Their dedication keeps AcreetionOS both stable and innovative.

We invite community members to test the latest kernel prepatch and provide feedback on the secret detection configuration, as early input helps us refine the workflow. Join the discussion on our Discord, GitLab, or Bluesky channels to share your insights and help shape upcoming releases.

Thank you for staying engaged with AcreetionOS; your support fuels our progress. Keep an eye on the download page for the next ISO release, and feel free to reach out with ideas or questions. Until next time, happy hacking and stay secure!
