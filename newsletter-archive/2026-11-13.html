Daily AcreetionOS Update -- November 13, 2026

November 13, 2026

AcreetionOS Newsletter - November 13, 2026

Top Development:

We're excited to announce that Rust 1.100.0 has been released, bringing notable changes including the stabilization of the std::sync module! This update is now available in our repositories, ensuring our users have access to the latest and greatest in Rust development. Our community has been hard at work ensuring a smooth transition, and we're grateful for their dedication.

Community News:

  • The AcreetionOS community has been actively contributing to various projects, including the development of Polo, a Wayland MATE compositor. This innovative project aims to provide a more seamless user experience for our users.
  • Our Discord server has seen a surge in activity, with users discussing everything from Linux development to their favorite strategy games. Join us to connect with like-minded individuals and stay up-to-date on the latest AcreetionOS news!

Practical Tip:

Did you know that you can easily update your AcreetionOS system using the sudo pacman -Syu command? This command will update your system to the latest version, ensuring you have the latest security patches and features. Simply open your terminal and type the command to get started!

That's all for this week's newsletter. Stay tuned for more updates from the AcreetionOS community, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need assistance. Happy hacking!
