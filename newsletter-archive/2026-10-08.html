Daily AcreetionOS Update -- October 08, 2026

October 08, 2026

October 08, 2026 Edition

Community News

AcreetionOS is proud to announce that our community has been actively contributing to the project's growth and development. We've seen a surge in new commits, issues, and pull requests on our GitLab instance. Our community's dedication to making AcreetionOS the best it can be is truly inspiring.

Practical Tip

Did you know that you can easily install AcreetionOS on a USB drive using our AcreetionMediaWriter tool? This allows you to try out our distribution without modifying your existing system. Simply download the tool from our website, follow the instructions, and you'll have AcreetionOS up and running on your USB drive in no time.

Recent Development Activity

The most interesting development this week is the addition of a new feature to our Calamares installer. Cobra3282000 has made significant changes to the installer, including forcing Wayland as the default desktop environment and installing lightdm-slick-greeter to fix display manager job crashes. This update will provide a smoother user experience for those using AcreetionOS.

Other notable developments:

  • Security updates have been issued by AlmaLinux for various packages, including bind, firefox, and kernel.
  • Natalie has made significant contributions to the project, including configuring Secret Detection in our GitLab CI/CD pipeline.
  • Cobra3282000 has also made several commits to the project, including fixing geocode-glib update conflicts and making changes to the Calamares config.

Get Involved

If you're interested in contributing to the AcreetionOS project, we encourage you to join our community on Discord or GitHub. Your contributions are invaluable to us, and we're always looking for talented individuals to help shape the future of our distribution.

Stay up-to-date

Follow us on social media to stay informed about the latest developments, news, and updates from the AcreetionOS community.

That's all for this week. Thank you for being part of the AcreetionOS community!
