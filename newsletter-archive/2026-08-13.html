Daily AcreetionOS Update - August 13, 2026

Dear AcreetionOS Community,

We're excited to share the latest updates from the AcreetionOS project with you. As we continue to work towards making our distribution the go-to choice for those seeking a lightweight, privacy-focused Linux experience, we're grateful for the support and contributions from our community.

In the past week, we've seen significant progress on various fronts. Our community has been actively discussing the importance of choosing the right hypervisor for testing OS ISOs, and we're glad to see the conversation happening. We're also happy to announce that QEMU 11.1 has been released, bringing with it over 3,200 commits and improvements to the emulator.

On the development side, Natalie has made some significant contributions, including the initial commit for the Linux App Drawer and the application menu search. Meanwhile, cobra3282000 has been working on resolving conflicts related to geocode-glib updates in both the AcreetionOS and AcreetionOS-XLibre repositories. These efforts will ensure that our distribution remains stable and secure.

We're also pleased to announce that our CI/CD pipelines are now publishing ISOs to the public server over SSH. This is a significant milestone, and we're grateful to Natalie for her work on this feature. Additionally, cobra3282000 has made changes to the package build process to fix errors related to glib geo stuff in AcreetionOS-XLibre.

As we move forward, we're committed to transparency and open communication. We're always looking for ways to improve our distribution and make it more user-friendly. If you have any suggestions or ideas, please don't hesitate to reach out to us on our Discord server or through our GitLab instance.

In other news, we're excited to see the progress being made on the HyperPick project, which aims to provide a simple and intuitive way to pick the right hypervisor for testing OS ISOs. We're grateful for the contributions from our community members and look forward to seeing the project come to fruition.

As always, we're grateful for the support of our community. Your contributions, whether it's through code, bug reports, or simply spreading the word about AcreetionOS, are invaluable to us. We're proud to be a part of this community, and we look forward to continuing to work together to make AcreetionOS the best it can be.

If you're new to AcreetionOS, we invite you to explore our documentation and get started with our distribution. We're confident that you'll find it to be a refreshing change from the usual Linux distributions out there. And if you're already a part of our community, thank you for your continued support. We're excited to see what the future holds for AcreetionOS.

Finally, we'd like to take a moment to acknowledge the hard work and dedication of our team members, including Natalie and cobra3282000. Your efforts are the backbone of this project, and we're grateful for your tireless work.

Thank you for being part of the AcreetionOS community. We're excited to see what the future holds for us, and we look forward to continuing to work together to make AcreetionOS the best it can be.

Best regards, Natalie Cole-Clift Spiva Co-Lead Developer, AcreetionOS Project
