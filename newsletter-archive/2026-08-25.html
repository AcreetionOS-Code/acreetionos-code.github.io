AcreetionOS Newsletter - August 25, 2026

Immutable A/B Profile and Versioned Release Automation

We're excited to announce the completion of our immutable A/B profile and versioned release automation in acreetionos-code/acreetionos-immutable. This feature allows us to create and manage multiple versions of our distribution, ensuring that users can easily switch between different profiles and configurations. With this update, we're taking a significant step towards making AcreetionOS even more flexible and user-friendly.

Community News

  • Our community has been busy, with several new commits and updates to our GitLab instance. Check out the recent activity feed for more details.
  • We're grateful for the contributions from our community members, including @cobra3282000, @natalie, and others who have helped shape AcreetionOS into what it is today.

Practical Tip

Did you know that you can easily create a bootable USB drive for AcreetionOS using our AcreetionMediaWriter tool? Simply download the tool from our website, follow the instructions, and you'll have a bootable USB drive ready to go. This is a great way to try out AcreetionOS without modifying your existing system.
