AcreetionOS Newsletter - September 03, 2026

Exciting News: Incus 7.4 Released!

We're thrilled to announce that Incus 7.4 has been released, marking a significant milestone in the world of container and virtual-machine management. This update brings numerous improvements and bug fixes, making it an excellent choice for users seeking a robust and reliable container management system. You can learn more about the release on the Linux Containers discussion forum.

Community News

Our community continues to grow and thrive, with many users contributing to the development and testing of AcreetionOS. We're grateful for the dedication and passion of our community members, who help shape the future of our distribution.

Practical Tip:

Did you know that you can easily install AcreetionOS on a USB drive using our AcreetionMediaWriter tool? This tool allows you to write AcreetionOS images to a USB drive from any operating system, making it a convenient way to try out our distribution without affecting your primary system. You can find more information on our website or in the AcreetionMediaWriter GitHub repository.

Stay Up-to-Date

To stay informed about the latest developments, be sure to follow us on our social media channels and join our Discord server. We're always excited to connect with our community and share updates on the AcreetionOS project.

Best regards, Natalie Cole-Clift Spiva Co-Lead Developer, AcreetionOS
