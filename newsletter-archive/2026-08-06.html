Daily AcreetionOS Update - August 06, 2026

Welcome to today's edition of the AcreetionOS newsletter. We're excited to bring you the latest developments from our small but dedicated team of builders working to create the most user-friendly Arch-based Linux distribution around. Natalie and Darren continue their daily grind of coding, testing, and occasionally breaking things in the name of progress.

Development Updates

We've made significant progress on our CI/CD pipeline recently. The team successfully configured GitLab to publish ISO builds directly to our public ISO server over SSH. This automation eliminates manual upload steps and ensures faster distribution of new builds to our community testers.

Security and code quality have been top priorities this week. We've added Secret Detection and SAST (Static Application Security Testing) configurations to our GitLab CI files. These tools will help us catch potential vulnerabilities and hard-coded secrets before they make it into production releases.

On the build system front, we've refined our ISO generation process. The ability to output builds to ~/Documents/iso_output/ on the host machine has been implemented, making it easier for developers to test locally without needing complex directory structures.

Community News

Our community continues to grow, with more users joining our Discord server each day. We've been seeing great discussions around desktop customization and hardware compatibility reports coming in from all corners of the globe. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us shape AcreetionOS into something truly special.

We want to give a special shoutout to everyone who's been submitting bug reports and feature requests. Your attention to detail and willingness to test beta builds has made our development cycle much more efficient. Keep those reports coming!

This week also saw the release of b4 0.16.0, a software-development tool that Konstantin Ryabitsev announced with some exciting new features. While not directly related to AcreetionOS development, staying current with useful tools helps us maintain our workflow efficiency.

Tips & Highlights

Did you know you can customize your AcreetionOS installation with different Plymouth themes for a personalized boot experience? The Cinnamon desktop environment makes it easy to tweak visual elements without touching terminal commands, though we always recommend backing up your configuration files first.

For those new to the distribution, our documentation team has been working hard to expand our FAQ section. Whether you're wondering about system requirements, installation tips, or just curious about our development philosophy, the answers are available at acreetionos.org.

If you're interested in contributing to the project, consider joining our GitLab instance where you can explore open issues and submit merge requests. We welcome contributions of all kinds, from code fixes to documentation improvements. Remember, even small changes can make a big difference in the overall user experience.

As always, thank you for being part of the AcreetionOS community. Your enthusiasm and support drive us to keep pushing forward, one commit at a time. Stay safe out there, and happy hacking!
