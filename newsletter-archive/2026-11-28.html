Daily AcreetionOS Update -- November 28, 2026

November 28, 2026

kms hook lands in AcreetionOS-Xlibre to fix NVIDIA no-signal boot

AcreetionOS-Xlibre just added a kms hook to its live initramfs config, finally fixing the NVIDIA no-signal boot that left release-1 testers staring at blank monitors.

Development The fix landed as a merge of main into release-1 in the AcreetionOS-Xlibre repository. By pulling the kms hook into the early initramfs, the open-source driver initializes before the proprietary NVIDIA module grabs the display, so the live ISO actually reaches a login screen on NVIDIA hardware. Darren Clift handles ISO builds and Calamares customization for the project, and this is exactly the kind of plumbing that makes the first boot feel less like gambling. If you have an NVIDIA card and a spare USB stick, the latest images are worth another spin at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html.

Community The wider Linux world had a big moment this week when Dave Täht received the Jonathan B. Postel Service Award from the Internet Society, recognized for work that made every millisecond count across the network stack. It is a good reminder that the routing and congestion control foundations underpinning Arch-based distributions are maintained by people who treat the internet like shared infrastructure. The full list of upstream security updates, including recent AlmaLinux and Debian advisories, is over at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html.

Tip of the day A TU Chemnitz mirror has been added and verified against origin SHA256SUMS, giving European users a closer option for package pulls. You can add it to your pacman mirrorlist and check the full mirror status on the wiki https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html. Meanwhile, the brand-new acreetionos-wallpapers repo made its initial commit, tracking backgrounds from the AcreetionOS GitLab instance if you want to refresh your Cinnamon desktop.
