Daily AcreetionOS Update -- September 28, 2026

September 28, 2026

CI pipeline fix ensures reliable AcreetionOS releases by stopping false "enforced" health checks in acreetionos-code.github.io

Development activity this week centered on tightening our release pipeline reliability. The team pushed commits to acreetionos-code.github.io addressing a health endpoint bug that was incorrectly reporting "enforced" status when verification secrets couldn't be validated. Additionally, the immutable build job now correctly passes -R flag to gh release create, eliminating the previous checkout dependency in the release workflow.

Community momentum continues to build as AcreetionOS gains recognition beyond our circles. BetaNews featured us as "It just works: AcreetionOS is the easy-to-use alternative to Windows 10/11," highlighting our user-friendly approach for Linux newcomers. Meanwhile, Shelly 3.1.5's expanded AUR support—covered by 9to5Linux and Linuxiac—means our users now have even better package management options through the graphical interface. These mentions reinforce why our community-driven development model works so well.

Quick tip for new users: Before flashing your ISO, verify the download signature using GPG. You can find the public key and verification instructions in our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html. This simple step ensures you're installing authentic, untampered AcreetionOS software.
