AcreetionOS Update — June 22, 2026

June 22, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has just been fine-tuned again, with recent changes making our release process even more reliable and efficient, and you can find all the details on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html. This is a great example of how our community-driven development process is always improving, and we're excited to see the impact it will have on our users.

Development The development team has been hard at work, with recent commits like the introduction of per-pipeline staging directories and relative symlinks for the latest ISO images, which are now live on our website, accessible via https://acreetionos.org/. We're also seeing some interesting projects popping up, like the initial release of configs and field notes from a DL380 G7 running Arch, which can be found on our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html.

Community Our community has been active as always, with discussions around the latest security updates and stable kernel sets, and we're grateful for the contributions and feedback we're receiving. If you're looking for ways to get involved, our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html is a great place to start, and don't forget to check out our flash page at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html for the latest news and updates.
