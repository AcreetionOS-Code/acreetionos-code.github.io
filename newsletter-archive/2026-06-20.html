Your System, Your Rules - Why Sovereignty Matters More Than Ever

June 20, 2026

Hey AcreetionOS Community,

Hope you're having a great weekend! Today we want to talk about something that's at the heart of everything we build here: system sovereignty.

In a world where more and more software phones home, tracks your habits, and makes decisions for you, having full control over your computer isn't just nice to have - it's essential. That's why AcreetionOS is built from the ground up with privacy and user autonomy as core principles, not afterthoughts.

Quick tip for our users: Want to see exactly what's connecting to the internet from your machine? Open a terminal and run ss -tunap. This shows all active network connections and which processes own them. Knowledge is power, and transparency is what makes Linux special. You won't find this kind of visibility on proprietary systems.

On the development front, we're hard at work refining the out-of-box experience. Our team is testing improvements to the welcome screen and first-run setup, making it even easier for newcomers to get comfortable with rolling release updates and system maintenance. The Arch base gives you cutting-edge software, and we're making sure it's approachable for everyone.

One thing we love about our community is how supportive it is. Whether you're migrating from Ubuntu, Fedora, or even Windows, our forums are full of folks ready to help you make the transition smooth. The Cinnamon desktop provides that familiar feel, while XLibre/X11 ensures rock-solid stability without the Wayland headaches some other distros are wrestling with.

Remember: every time you choose free, open-source software that respects your freedom, you're voting for a better digital future.

Stay sovereign,

The AcreetionOS Team
