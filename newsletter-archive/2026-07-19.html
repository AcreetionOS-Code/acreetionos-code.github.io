AcreetionOS Update — July 19, 2026

July 19, 2026

A significant milestone has been achieved in our continuous integration pipeline with the implementation of per-pipeline staging directories to avoid publish races between concurrent builds, and relative symlinks for the latest.iso file, making our ISO build process more reliable. This improvement is a direct result of our community's efforts to refine and stabilize our workflow, ensuring that our users receive the most up-to-date and stable versions of AcreetionOS.

Development The recent updates to our continuous integration pipeline have been a major focus for our community, with the goal of creating a more efficient and reliable ISO build process. As outlined in our changelog at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html, these changes are crucial in maintaining the stability and security of AcreetionOS. Our community's dedication to improving the workflow is a testament to the collaborative spirit of our project.

Community The AcreetionOS community has been actively engaged in discussions and contributions, with recent commits and updates reflecting the community's efforts to improve the project. For more information on how to get involved and contribute to AcreetionOS, please visit our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html. Additionally, our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html provides a wealth of information on various topics related to AcreetionOS.
