AcreetionOS Update — June 13, 2026

June 13, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has been fine-tuned once again, with the latest improvement ensuring that the latest ISO images resolve correctly on the host web root using relative symlinks, and this change is now documented on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html. This enhancement, combined with the recent introduction of per-pipeline staging directories, significantly boosts the reliability and efficiency of our release process.

Development The development team continues to work on refining the ISO build pipeline, with recent commits focusing on avoiding publish races between concurrent builds and ensuring that the latest ISO images are always accessible. You can read more about the details of these improvements on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html, and explore our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html for a deeper dive into the project's inner workings.

Community The community has been active in discussing the latest updates, including the release of Emacs 31.1 and recent security updates. You can join the conversation and stay up-to-date on the latest developments by visiting our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html, where you'll find guides, tutorials, and other resources to help you get the most out of AcreetionOS. For the latest news and updates, be sure to check out our flash page at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html, and don't forget to visit our main website at https://acreetionos.org/ for an overview of the project and its goals.
