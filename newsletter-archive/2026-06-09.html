AcreetionOS Update — June 09, 2026

June 09, 2026

Recent improvements to our continuous integration pipeline have significantly reduced the likelihood of publish races between concurrent builds, ensuring that our users receive the latest ISO images in a timely and reliable manner. This change, which utilizes per-pipeline staging directories, is a key step forward in maintaining the stability and integrity of our release process.

Development The development team has been actively reviewing recent upstream activity around Arch Linux and related tools, such as the latest Emacs 31.1 release, which brings a long list of changes including the removal of the old Emacs calendar. You can learn more about our development progress and the latest changes on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html.

Community Our community has been actively discussing the latest security updates, including those issued by AlmaLinux for packages such as ansible-core, cups-filters, and curl. We encourage all users to stay up to date with the latest security patches and to report any issues they may encounter. For more information on how to keep your system secure, you can visit our documentation page at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html. Additionally, you can find more detailed information about our project on our wiki page at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html.

Tip of the day If you're looking for the latest ISO image, you can find it on our website at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html. We also recommend checking out our documentation page for tips on how to get the most out of your AcreetionOS system. By following these tips and staying up to date with the latest changes, you can help ensure that your system remains stable, secure, and running smoothly.
