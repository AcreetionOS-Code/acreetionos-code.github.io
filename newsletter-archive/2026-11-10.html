AcreetionOS Newsletter - November 10, 2026

Breaking News: OpenIndiana 2026.10 Released!

We're excited to announce that OpenIndiana 2026.10 has been released, featuring a range of improvements and bug fixes. This release includes updates to the kernel, LibreOffice, Rsync, and more. You can read the full announcement on the OpenIndiana website.

Community News

  • Our community has been hard at work, and we're proud to see the progress being made on various projects. The kernel prepatch 7.4-rc2 has been released, and security updates have been issued by Debian and Fedora.
  • We're also seeing some great contributions to our codebase, including a fix for the Flash page mobile responsiveness issue and a welcome workflow added to our CI/CD pipeline.

Practical Tip: Upgrading to AcreetionOS-Xlibre

Are you looking to upgrade to the latest version of AcreetionOS-Xlibre? Make sure to run the following command in your terminal:

``bash sudo pacman -Syyu ``

This will update your package list and upgrade your system to the latest version of AcreetionOS-Xlibre.

Stay Connected

Join our community on Discord to stay up-to-date with the latest news and developments. We're always happy to have new members join and contribute to the project.
