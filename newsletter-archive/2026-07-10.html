AcreetionOS Update — July 10, 2026

July 10, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has become even more robust with the latest enhancements, including per-pipeline staging directories and relative symlinks for the latest.iso file, significantly improving the reliability of our ISO build process. These updates are a direct result of our community's tireless efforts to refine and stabilize our workflow, ensuring that AcreetionOS remains a cutting-edge, Arch-based Linux distribution.

Development The recent updates to our CI pipeline are a testament to the community's dedication to improving the overall quality and stability of AcreetionOS. With these enhancements, we can ensure that our ISO builds are more reliable and efficient, allowing us to focus on other areas of development, such as refining our documentation and wiki guides, which can be found at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html and https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html.

Community The release of Emacs 31.1 has generated significant interest within our community, with many users exploring its new features and improvements. We encourage all users to share their experiences and tips on using Emacs 31.1 with AcreetionOS on our forums and wiki pages. For the latest news and updates on AcreetionOS, including security updates and kernel releases, such as the recent stable kernel set, visit https://acreetionos.org/ and https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html.

Tip of the day To stay up-to-date with the latest developments and security updates, we recommend regularly checking our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html and our flash page at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html for important announcements and updates. By doing so, you can ensure that your AcreetionOS installation remains secure and up-to-date, and you can take advantage of the latest features and improvements.
