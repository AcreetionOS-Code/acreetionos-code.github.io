Daily AcreetionOS Update -- September 30, 2026

September 30, 2026

September 29 intelligence sync upgrades AcreetionOS newsletter and wiki

AcreetionOS just shipped a fresh intelligence sync that polished the newsletter archive, wiki guides, and RSS feed on September 29, marking the project's most thorough content overhaul of the year.

Development The sync landed as commit 1711 in acreetionos-code.github.io, pushing updated stories, archived issues, and refined wiki pages into production. Natalie (@spivanatalie64) and Darren (@cobra3282000) coordinated the pull, ensuring the changelog https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html now reflects the latest fixes and the download portal https://acreetionos.org/flash.html points to a stabilized build. This pass also tightened the internal link network, so the new docs landing at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html is instantly reachable from every recent post.

Community Archinstall 4.5 arrived with native AArch64 support, GRUB/Limine integration, and real‑time kernel options, giving Arch users a smoother path to ARM installations. Phoronix and 9to5Linux highlighted the release, and the community is already swapping tips on the AcreetionOS Discord. Meanwhile, a warning buzzes about over 400 AUR packages being hijacked to drop infostealers and eBPF rootkits—users are advised to audit any new PKGBUILDs before building.

Tip of the day When you pull the latest ISO from https://acreetionos.org/flash.html, run calamares with the “--quiet” flag to skip the splash animation; it cuts boot time by a few seconds and leaves you more time for exploring the new wiki entries.

Visit the homepage https://acreetionos.org/ to see the refreshed layout and dive into the updated guides that now accompany every release.
