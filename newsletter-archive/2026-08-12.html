Daily AcreetionOS Update - August 12, 2026

Development work continues at a steady pace across the AcreetionOS codebase with several important fixes implemented this week. Darren Clift has been focusing on resolving dependency conflicts, specifically addressing a critical geocode-glib update issue that was causing problems on installed systems. The fix has been merged into both the main acreetionos repository and the acreetionos-xlibre branch, ensuring users won't encounter this particular update conflict moving forward.

New experimental projects are taking shape in the development environment, including initial commits for a Linux application drawer and application menu search functionality. These early-stage projects suggest we're laying groundwork for enhanced user experience features, though details remain limited at this preliminary stage. Work continues on hyperpick, our custom hypervisor selection tool for testing OS ISOs, which now has its initial code structure in place.

The build pipeline has seen improvements with CI/CD optimizations that ensure ISO images are published more reliably to our public server. This means faster turnaround times for community members testing development snapshots and release candidates. Both Natalie and Darren have been iterating on the installer customizations, keeping our Calamares-based installation experience smooth and user-friendly.

Community contributions remain robust, with several interesting discussions highlighting broader Linux ecosystem developments. GNOME Shell design contributor Tobias Bernard has published thoughts on future interface directions that may inform our own desktop experience planning. Security updates continue to flow from upstream distributions including AlmaLinux, Debian, and Fedora, reminding us of the importance of staying current with patches.

Our community Discord server (discord.gg/VHqQkJASw7) remains active with real-time discussions, and we're seeing engagement around ISO testing and troubleshooting. The community has been particularly helpful in beta testing recent installer improvements, providing feedback that directly shapes our development priorities. GitLab activity shows consistent maintenance across our sovereign infrastructure.

For users running AcreetionOS, you should have received the geocode-glib fix through regular system updates if you have our repositories configured. This addresses the specific conflict that arose from recent library updates and ensures your system remains stable during routine maintenance. As always, no telemetry collects from your installation and all updates respect your privacy.

Looking ahead, the application menu search feature appears to be moving toward early testing phases, which could significantly improve how users find their programs. Darren's ongoing work with XLibre integration continues to progress, with recent commits showing adjustments to package builds that should resolve the glib geo compatibility issues. The Calamares installer maintenance remains a priority to ensure flawless installation experiences.

The two-person development team, Natalie and Darren, continues to manage all aspects of this Arch-based distribution with dedication to creating a stable, privacy-respecting desktop experience. Their complementary skills—Natalie's frontend focus and Darren's infrastructure expertise—enable rapid iteration while maintaining the quality our community expects. This collaborative father-daughter partnership drives the unique character of AcreetionOS.

Thank you to everyone who supports AcreetionOS through feedback, testing, and contributions. For questions or to get involved, visit our website at acreetionos.org, join us on Discord, or follow our progress on X and Bluesky. We appreciate your continued trust in this community-driven, user-friendly Arch Linux distribution.
