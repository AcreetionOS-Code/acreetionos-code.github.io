Daily AcreetionOS Update -- September 09, 2026

September 09, 2026

AcreetionOS just cleaned up the installer for older Nvidia GPUs and removed mandatory disk encryption, letting users choose later. The change, made in cobra3282000/calamares-config, fixes driver compatibility and lets you postpone encryption setup until you’re ready. It’s a small but big win for anyone rocking legacy graphics cards or wanting a quicker first boot. Check the https://acreetionos.org/ homepage for the updated installer details.

Development / The fix lands in cobra3282000/calamares-config, where the team swapped out the strict disk‑encryption prompt and added a fallback 1080p gfxmode for older Nvidia cards. The commit also pins v0.3.0 archive checksums in natalie/us-infra-hub and adds an MCP stdio server for future automation. If you’re curious about the full story, the changelog at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html lists every tweak.

Community / The community push of the bash2bat conversion script (initial push by a new contributor) and the newly configured Secret Detection in .gitlab-ci.yml (in natalie/my-politecal-stances) show the project’s growing security focus. Meanwhile, the recent Jellyfin 12.0 release is a favorite among media enthusiasts, and the SAST configuration in .gitlab-ci.yml is already protecting new code. Join the conversation on the wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html to see how you can help.

Tip of the day / Want to stay ahead of encryption options? Use the AcreetionMediaWriter at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html to create a USB with the “no‑encryption” profile, then follow the quick guide in the docs at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html to enable encryption later without a full reinstall. Happy hacking!
