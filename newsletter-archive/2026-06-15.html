AcreetionOS Update — June 15, 2026

June 15, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has just been fine-tuned again, with the latest improvement ensuring that the latest ISO images resolve correctly on the host web root using relative symlinks, a change that is now documented on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html. This comes on the heels of the introduction of per-pipeline staging directories, which significantly boosts the reliability and efficiency of our release process.

Development The development team has been hard at work refining the pipeline to avoid publish races between concurrent builds, and the results are already showing in terms of smoother and more reliable releases. You can read more about the details of these changes on our changelog page at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html, and learn how to get the most out of AcreetionOS on our documentation page at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html.

Community The community has been actively discussing the latest Emacs 31.1 release and its implications for AcreetionOS users, and we encourage everyone to join in on the conversation. Meanwhile, our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html is being continually updated with new guides and tutorials to help users get the most out of their AcreetionOS experience. For the latest news and updates, be sure to check out our flash page at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html, and visit our main website at https://acreetionos.org/ for more information on the project.
