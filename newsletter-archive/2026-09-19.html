Daily AcreetionOS Update -- September 19, 2026

September 19, 2026

The geocode-glib update that had been blocking upgrades on installed AcreetionOS systems is now resolved after a quick merge of the repair branch. Users can safely run their regular pacman syncs again without hitting the transaction error that appeared earlier this week.

Development The fix came through a community‑driven merge request that reverted the conflicting geocode-glib version and added a version pin to prevent the issue from recurring. The updated packages are already in the stable repository, and the changelog notes the exact commit IDs for those who like to follow the source. If you haven’t yet, pull the latest sync and enjoy a smooth upgrade once more.

Community Discussion on the #support channel of the Discord server helped pinpoint the root cause, with reports from longtime members like spivanatalie64 and cobra3282000 confirming the problem on multiple hardware setups. At the same time, the broader Arch ecosystem is grappling with a wave of malicious AUR packages, so the team reminds everyone to verify PKGBUILD sources and to consider using the official acreetionos repos for critical tools. The solidarity shown in troubleshooting this glitch highlights how the project’s volunteer spirit keeps the distro reliable.

Tip of the day After applying the geocode-glib fix, take a moment to review your pacman cache with pacman -Qk to ensure no stray files linger from the interrupted transaction. For a clean slate, you can also download the latest ISO from the flash page and test it in a virtual machine before committing to a bare‑metal install. Keeping your system in sync with the official repos remains the easiest way to stay ahead of future hiccups.
