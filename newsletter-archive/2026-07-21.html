AcreetionOS Update — July 21, 2026

July 21, 2026

The AcreetionOS continuous integration pipeline has just been further refined with the implementation of per-pipeline staging directories and relative symlinks for the latest.iso file, making our ISO build process even more reliable and efficient. This improvement is a direct result of our community's efforts to refine and stabilize our workflow, ensuring that our users receive the most up-to-date and stable versions of AcreetionOS, which can be easily accessed and learned about through our documentation at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html.

Development The recent updates to our continuous integration pipeline have significantly improved the reliability and efficiency of our ISO build process, allowing us to provide our users with the latest and most stable versions of AcreetionOS. Our community's efforts to refine and stabilize our workflow are ongoing, and we encourage all users to check out our changelog at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html to stay up-to-date with the latest developments.

Community The AcreetionOS community has been actively engaged in discussions and contributions, with recent topics including the release of Emacs 31.1, which is now available to AcreetionOS users through the Arch repositories, and can be easily installed using pacman. Our community is also exploring various configurations and setups, such as the DL380 G7 running Arch, with field notes and configs available for reference. For more information on getting involved with the community, visit our wiki at https://acreetionos.org/wiki.html.

Tip of the day To stay current with the latest security updates, such as those issued on Monday, users can check our website at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html for the latest news and updates. Additionally, our ISO build pipeline is designed to provide the most up-to-date and stable versions of AcreetionOS, and users can learn more about our development process and how to get involved through our documentation and community resources.
