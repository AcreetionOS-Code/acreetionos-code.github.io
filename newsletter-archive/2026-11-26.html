Daily AcreetionOS Update -- November 26, 2026

November 26, 2026

Subject: Bottom-bar icons are clickable again in acreetionos and acreetionos-xlibre

The panel icons that refused to open on click are fixed, and the patch landed in both acreetionos and acreetionos-xlibre — commit message included the smiley, so you know it was personal. If you've been right-clicking your way around the taskbar for the past week, update and it should behave.

Development

Darren spent the day in the unglamorous corners. Alongside the bottom-bar fix he got the welcome screen back up after missing pieces had knocked it out ("so its back :)"), and he cleared two screen bugs and a resizing problem out of fences. Natalie opened a brand-new repo, acreetionos-wallpapers, with its initial commit — which means the desktop is about to get some attention, not just the plumbing. Full commit notes live on the changelog at https://acreetionos.org/changelog.html.

Community

Two things from the wider ecosystem worth your coffee break. The Internet Society gave Dave Täht the Jonathan B. Postel Service Award for his bufferbloat work — the reason your router doesn't melt under load is partly his fault, in the best way. And System76 shipped Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS. We're a two-person Arch shop, so we don't agree with every distro decision, but a healthy Linux desktop ecosystem is good for all of us.

Tip of the day

If a panel or screen bug survives an update, clear the stale state before you file anything: log out, remove the cached panel config in your home directory, and log back in. Nine times out of ten the fix was shipping fine and something old was still holding the seat. If it genuinely persists, the docs at https://acreetionos.org/docs.html cover where your config lives, and the Discord is the fastest way to reach us — Darren answers faster than the CI does.

New here? Grab an ISO at https://acreetionos.org/flash.html.
