Breaking News: Firefox Fix Package Added to AcreetionOS

We are excited to announce that a new package has been added to AcreetionOS to address a long-standing issue with Firefox window position restoration on Linux. The firefox-fix package, contributed by cobra3282000, ensures that Firefox windows retain their position even after system restarts. This update is now available for download and will be included in the next AcreetionOS release.

Practical Tip: Customizing Your AcreetionOS Experience

Did you know that you can customize your AcreetionOS experience to suit your needs? By editing the ~/.config/calamares/config file, you can change the default desktop environment to Wayland or modify other settings to your liking. This is just one example of the flexibility and customization options available in AcreetionOS. Experiment and find the perfect setup for your workflow!
