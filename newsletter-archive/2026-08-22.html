AcreetionOS Newsletter - August 22, 2026

KDE Gear 26.08 Released

We're excited to announce that KDE Gear 26.08 has been released, bringing new features and improvements to the KDE ecosystem. As a community-driven Linux distribution, we're committed to staying up-to-date with the latest developments in the open-source world. This release includes updates to various KDE applications, including Dolphin, Gwenview, and Krita, among others.

Community News

  • Our community has been busy with various projects, including the development of new packages and updates to our documentation. We're grateful for the hard work and dedication of our contributors, who help make AcreetionOS a better distribution for everyone.
  • We've also seen an increase in user engagement on our Discord server, with many users sharing their experiences and feedback with the community. If you're not already a member, consider joining us to connect with other users and developers.

Practical Tip

Did you know that you can customize your AcreetionOS desktop with our extensive collection of themes and icons? Our community has created a wide range of options to suit different tastes and preferences. To access the theme and icon manager, simply search for "AcreetionOS Themes" in your package manager and install the package. From there, you can browse and install your preferred themes and icons to give your desktop a personalized touch.

That's all for this week's newsletter. Stay tuned for more updates, and thank you for being part of the AcreetionOS community!
