Our guarantees -- no ads, no identifiable cookies, we don't want to know who you are

AcreetionOS will never serve ads on this website or in the operating system. No ad slots, no sponsored placements, no selling of attention. Period.

We refuse any cookie or storage that identifies you. We don't want to know who you are -- only whether a page or mirror helped (aggregate counts, not people). Any tag that would set an identifiable cookie, fingerprint you, or track you across sites will be refused in GTM container review. Ahrefs is cookie-free by design; GA4 (if consented) is configured for anonymized, non-identifiable measurement only -- and if it can't be made anonymous, it won't be used.

We are adding GTM + Ahrefs only for compliance (GDPR / UK GDPR / ePrivacy consent-gating, auditability) and relationship-building (which docs and mirrors are useful, in aggregate). This guarantee is part of #668 and is enforced in GTM policy.

TL;DR: This site uses Ahrefs Web Analytics (cookie-free, aggregate) and, when enabled, Google Tag Manager (GTM) to load strictly compliance & relationship-building tags only (e.g. GA4 page/conversion measurement). No ads. All GTM tags are gated by Consent Mode v2 (default denied) -- nothing fires until you consent. Block the scripts with a content blocker or use the consent banner to withdraw at any time. See How to opt out.

2. Why we measure -- compliance & relationship-building only

We measure for two reasons only: compliance (a single, auditable place to enforce Consent Mode v2, honor GDPR/UK GDPR/ePrivacy, and document what loads) and relationship-building (which docs, mirrors, and release notes actually help people, where downloads fail, what to improve next) -- without editing 80+ HTML files for every tag. GTM gives that single auditable place to add, gate, and disable tags.

What we don't do -- and never will

  • No ads -- ever. No ad slots, no sponsored placements, no ad-serving tags in GTM.
  • No identifiable cookies -- ever. We refuse any cookie/storage that identifies you. We don't want to know who you are; we measure pages, not people (aggregate only).
  • No OS telemetry -- AcreetionOS itself collects nothing (see Privacy).
  • No selling or sharing of analytics data.
  • No fingerprinting or cross-site tracking -- refused by container policy, even under consent.

4. U.S. compliance disclosure -- CCPA/CPRA & state privacy laws

We apply the same anonymous, no-identification standard to U.S. visitors -- and we meet U.S. disclosure and opt-out duties.

What U.S. law requires here

  • CCPA as amended by CPRA (California) + CalOPPA -- we disclose categories collected (aggregate page analytics, not personal identifiers), purposes (compliance & aggregate relationship-building), retention, and that we do not sell or share personal information and do not use/share sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary. No sale/share means no "Do Not Sell/Share" sale to opt out of, but we still honor your opt-out signals.
  • State privacy laws -- Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Utah (UCPA), Texas (TDPSA), Oregon (OCPA), Montana, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Indiana and others -- we honor the rights they grant where you live: right to know/access, delete, correct (where applicable), opt out of sale/share/targeted advertising, and non-discrimination for exercising rights.
  • FTC Act / common law -- no deceptive tracking; this disclosure and the consent banner are the notice and choice mechanism.

Your U.S. rights -- how to exercise

  • Do Not Sell or Share: we do not sell or share personal information (as defined by CCPA/CPRA). You don't need to opt out of a sale that doesn't happen -- blocking the analytics scripts (see §5) guarantees it.
  • Opt out of analytics: use the opt-out controls in §5 (Reject in the consent banner, or block analytics.ahrefs.com / googletagmanager.com). The site remains fully usable.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC): we honor GPC as an opt-out of any sale/share and as a request to treat you as having opted out of analytics where GPC is a valid signal under CCPA/CPRA and Colorado CPA. No separate form needed.
  • Know / delete / correct: because we collect only aggregate, non-identifiable counts (and refuse identifiable cookies -- see §3), there is typically no personal information about you to know/delete/correct. If you believe we hold something identifiable, contact us -- we respond within 45 days (CCPA/CPRA timeline) and we will not discriminate against you for exercising rights.
  • Minors: this site is not directed to children under 13 (COPPA) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. How to opt out / withdraw consent

  • Consent banner: choose Reject or open Manage preferences to toggle Analytics / Ads (Consent Mode v2). Re-open via the footer link or the banner's gear icon.
  • Content blocker: block analytics.ahrefs.com and googletagmanager.com / google-analytics.com -- the site remains fully usable.
  • Browser: enable Do Not Track / GPC where honored, or clear cookies for this site; GTM respects ad_storage/analytics_storage denied.
