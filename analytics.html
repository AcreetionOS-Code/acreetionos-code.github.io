Our guarantee — no ads, ever

AcreetionOS will never serve ads on this website or in the operating system. No ad slots, no sponsored placements, no selling of attention. Period.

We are adding Google Tag Manager + Ahrefs only for compliance (GDPR / UK GDPR / ePrivacy consent-gating, auditability) and relationship-building (understanding which docs, mirrors, and release notes actually help people, and keeping community communication relevant). If a tag would enable ad serving, it will not be added. This guarantee is part of #668 and will be enforced in GTM container policy.

TL;DR: This site uses Ahrefs Web Analytics (cookie-free, aggregate) and, when enabled, Google Tag Manager (GTM) to load strictly compliance & relationship-building tags only (e.g. GA4 page/conversion measurement). No ads. All GTM tags are gated by Consent Mode v2 (default denied) — nothing fires until you consent. Block the scripts with a content blocker or use the consent banner to withdraw at any time. See How to opt out.

2. Why we measure — compliance & relationship-building only

We measure for two reasons only: compliance (a single, auditable place to enforce Consent Mode v2, honor GDPR/UK GDPR/ePrivacy, and document what loads) and relationship-building (which docs, mirrors, and release notes actually help people, where downloads fail, what to improve next) — without editing 80+ HTML files for every tag. GTM gives that single auditable place to add, gate, and disable tags.

What we don't do — and never will

  • No ads — ever. No ad slots, no sponsored placements, no ad-serving tags in GTM. This is a binding guarantee for this site and the OS.
  • No OS telemetry — AcreetionOS itself collects nothing (see Privacy).
  • No selling or sharing of analytics data.
  • No fingerprinting or cross-site tracking beyond what the listed processors do under explicit consent.

3. Cookies, storage & data

Controller
AcreetionOS (community project) — contact via contact page.
Processors
Ahrefs Pte. Ltd. (Ahrefs Analytics); Google LLC (GTM + GA4/Ads as configured).
Legal basis
Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) / UK GDPR, ePrivacy) for GTM/GA4/Ads; legitimate interest for aggregate, cookie-free Ahrefs page counts where consent not required — migrated to consent-gated via GTM.
Data categories
Ahrefs: aggregate page views, referrer, country/region, device/browser family (no IP stored with analytics). GA4 (if consented): page events, approximate location, device, consent-aware session IDs; IP truncated/anonymized per Google settings.
Cookies / storage
Ahrefs: none (cookie-free). GTM/GA4/Ads (if consented): _ga, _ga_<ID>, _gcl_*, and related localStorage — only after granted.
Retention
Ahrefs: per Ahrefs policy (aggregate). GA4: 2 / 14 months (configurable; disclosed when finalized). GTM itself stores no analytics data.
Transfers
Google may process in the US; Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) apply where required. Ahrefs: Singapore.

4. How to opt out / withdraw consent

  • Consent banner: choose Reject or open Manage preferences to toggle Analytics / Ads (Consent Mode v2). Re-open via the footer link or the banner's gear icon.
  • Content blocker: block analytics.ahrefs.com and googletagmanager.com / google-analytics.com — the site remains fully usable.
  • Browser: enable Do Not Track / GPC where honored, or clear cookies for this site; GTM respects ad_storage/analytics_storage denied.
